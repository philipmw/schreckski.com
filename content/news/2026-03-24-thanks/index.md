If you want to be ready for next season XC skiing, well here's some ideas. Skip if your not interested in improving your XC technique:

1. Go to my website Schreckski.com. As there are links to my YouTube teaching videos for skate (15) and classic skiing (10). Put on some socks, spray your wood flooring with Pledge and ski along with me. Like it and subscribe!
2.  Ski bound.  Find a good hill with a softer surface: Like Tiger or Cougar Mountain.  Using your poles, just bound or run 🏃‍♀️ Try using your poles like you would skiing.  Good for upper body and balance.
3. At a gym 🏋️‍♀️ or with cords at home, do pull downs but on one leg 🦵 or back and forth mimicking a specific ski technique. Do squats,  dead lifts, etc. Compound exercises that work your key muscles in core, legs and upper back. These can be single leg exercises for balance. 
4. Rollerskiing or Rollerblading, both with poles.  Be safe! A quiet cul-de-sac or bike trail with padding on every part of your body you want safe: helmet, gloves, elbow,  knee and pelvis pads!
5. Okay Schreck, I have a real job/career and kids!  None of that works in my busy schedule. 

So here's something that takes no extra time.  When you brush your teeth twice a day, ski! Balance on one 🦵 at a time. It works and takes no extra time! Best use of 2 minutes for you!
