Nov 2025 Christmas 🎄 Ski lessons under the Christmas 🎄

Hi XC skiers,

First, keep up your snow dances. Lots of Snow is forecasted for this upcoming Turkey Week on Snoqualmie Pass. The more snow, the earlier we get to XC ski in December!

I have been getting lots of requests for FAMILY beginning Christmas and birthday XC ski lessons with me again. Please make sure your kids or spouse really wants to learn how to Classic or Skate ski before you sign them up! I promise to help them love winter and maybe even XC skiing with you!

If you have beginning skiers in your family, try starting them with Classic XC ski lessons (with me. I cannot guarantee success teaching your partner!) I recommend renting ski gear for their XC ski lessons. You can gift ski equipment next Christmas if they ask Santa!

For those in Seattle, probably rent with REI. For East Sider XC riders, I am impressed with Pro Ski Service in North Bend at 112 on W. 2nd. St. MIKE YOST is a Great XC boot fitter and they get the ski and pole sizing right. Ask for an XC Skier to help you at either location. Snow boarders are great...if you want to board! Their general store info #425-888-6397.

Also Pro Ski is a great local place to get your new gear. If you are traveling North, Winthrop Mountain Sports in Winthrop is awesome if you have a vacation in the Methow Valley! You can also buy from them via a phone or Zoom call. Ask for Marine or Erik Bjornsen, the owners and retired World Cup Ski racers. The phone # 509-846-3265. Or marine@winthropmountainsports.com

Please tell Mike, Marine or Erik that Schreck sent you! They are really nice skiers to work with and they put up with me. I buy all my gear from Marine and Erik during my December trip to the XC mecca, the Methow Valley.
